================================================================================
ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Input: LearnerProfile
  ↓
[Skill Gap Analysis] → SkillGapResult
  ↓ (identifies missing skills & priorities)
[Recommendation Engine] + RAG-provided resources → Recommendations
  ↓ (ranks resources with explanations)
[Learning Path Generator] → LearningPath
  ↓ (orders respecting prerequisites)
Output: Personalized learning recommendations & path
